How To Use The App

1 🔑

Login

Type your Username and Password. Tap the Login button.

2 ➕

Add New Loan

Tap New Loan. Fill customer, loan and vehicle details. Tap Submit.

3 ✅

Approval

Admin checks the loan and taps Approve. This creates the EMI schedule automatically.

4 💳

Pay EMI

Open Loans → EMI. Type the Bill Number and Amount. Tap Pay.

5 🔔

Check Alerts

Open Alerts daily to see who is overdue or due soon. Send SMS reminders from here.

6 👥

Customers

See every customer's loan, vehicle and payment status in one list.

7 📞

Follow Up

If a customer needs more time, tap Follow Up and pick a new date to collect.

8 📊

Reports

Open Report to download loan details as PDF or Excel anytime.

⭐ Good To Know

  • 🧾A Bill Number is compulsory for every payment, even part-payments.
  • 🚗Vehicle details are needed only when Reloan = Yes.
  • 📅You can change the Paid On date if you are recording a late entry.
  • 🔒Always tap Logout when you finish using a shared computer.
